A class (also called lecture) generally is a discussion of one or more Sanskrit verses from Vaisnava literature like the Bhagavad-gita, Srimad-bhagavatam and other books by A.C. Bhaktivedante Swami Prabhupada (ISKCON, BBT) and other acaryas (guru’s). A recording of a class generally follow a set structure, although each recording can differ. 

## Recording structure before editing 

Before a class is edited (by the "Class Trimmer Tool") the structure could like this:

1. Introduction mantra (starts with "Om namo bhagavate Vasudeva" or "Jaya jaya sri caitanya")
2. Book and verse introduction (starts with "We are reading from...."
3. Sanskrit verse (audience repeats)
   - can be repeated 3x times
4. Sanskrit verse by audience member
   - can be repeated by audience
5. Synonyms (word by word english translation of verse)
   - audience repeats each synonym
6. English verse translation
7. Purport
8. Oblations (starts with "om Ajnana timaranda")
9. Explanation and discussion of the purport (the actual class)
10. Questions and answers

## Recording structure after editing
After the recording is edited (by the class trimmer tool) the structure should have this structure

2. Book and verse introdction 
3. 1x sanskrit verse by speaker
5. Synonyms
6. English verse translation
7. Purport
9. Explanation and discussion of the purport (the actual class)
10. Questions and answers


### exceptions 
- Sometimes, due to time pressure or other factors the speaker can skip the introduction, word for word and oblations.
- Generally only 1 verse is discussed, but some recordings have multiple verses
  - the speaker can decide to discuss a small part or break the purport into segments
