## Introduction
A class (also called lecture) is a discussion of one or more Sanskrit verses from Vaisnava literature like the Bhagavad-gita, Srimad-bhagavatam and other books by A.C. Bhaktivedante Swami Prabhupada (ISKCON, BBT) and other acaryas (guru’s). During live recording in ISKCON temples, the whole class is captured. But edited classes should have certain sections trimmed out. Most unedited recordings follow a certain structure and usually contain a set of sections, but each recording is unique. An edited class should only retain the most important sections.

## Unedited Recording sections
And unedited recording usually has these sections:

1. Introduction mantra (starts with "Om namo bhagavate Vasudeva" or "Jaya jaya sri caitanya")
2. Book and verse introduction 
3. Sanskrit verse (audience repeats)
   - can be repeated 3x times
4. Sanskrit verse by audience member
   - can be repeated by audience
5. Synonyms (word by word english translation of verse)
   - audience repeats each synonym
6. English verse translation
7. Purport
8. Oblations (starts with "om Ajnana timaranda")
9. Explanation and discussion of the purport (the actual class)
10. Questions and answers

### exceptions 
- Sometimes, due to time pressure or other factors, the speaker can skip the introduction, word for word and oblations.
- Generally only 1 verse is discussed, but some recordings have a verse sequence
  - the speaker can decide to discuss a small part or break the purport into segments


## Edited recording sections
After editing these sections should remain in the recording:

2. Book and verse introduction 
3. 1x sanskrit verse by speaker
5. Synonyms
6. English verse translation
7. Purport
9. Explanation and discussion of the purport (the actual class)
10. Questions and answers
